## Thursday: Recruiting tips
Making a college baseball recruiting video?
DON'T:
❌ Start with a long intro
❌ Use music over everything
❌ Hide your jersey number
❌ Include only your best play
DO:
✅ Show your position immediately
✅ Use clear, steady footage
✅ Include relevant game footage
✅ Make it easy for coaches to evaluate you

## Tuesday: Recruiting education
Pitchers: Velocity matters. But it's not everything.
College coaches also evaluate:
⚾ Command
⚾ Secondary pitches
⚾ Delivery and athleticism
⚾ Ability to get hitters out
⚾ Projection and development
The goal isn't just to throw hard. It's to get outs.
#Pitching #CollegeBaseball

## Thursday: Recruiting tips
